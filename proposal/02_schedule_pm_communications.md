Project Timeline: Award through December 2031, with buffer at each gate. Dates follow the RFTP's notional timeline off a September 15, 2026 award.

Setup and Launch (award to registration): Six weeks to finalize rules with NCATS and NASA BPS, build the website, and seat the reviewer roster. Registration opens October 25, 2026, the NIH Challenge Webinar follows in November, and Stage 1 submissions run December 31, 2026 to July 2027, with up to 25 winners announced November 2027.

Stage 2 (December 2027 to March 2030): The submission period opens December 2027. Milestone 1 demonstrations are due July 2028, up to 15 winners announced November 2028. Milestone 2 prototypes are due November 2029, with six runners-up and the grand prize winner announced March 2030.

Convening and Closeout (May 2030 to December 2031): Winners present May 2030; all solutions are available by May 31, 2030 per RFTP 3.1. The twelve-month post-award survey and final report close the task order December 31, 2031. Fixed lead times protect each date: rules and rubrics approved before a round opens, reviewer recruitment and conflict screening 21 days before close, scoring and reconciliation within 30 days.

Key risks are low Stage 1 participation in a community still emergent at the nexus of the problem, judges credible in both fields, and drift across five years.
