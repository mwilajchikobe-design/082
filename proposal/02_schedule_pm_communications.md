RISK. Three risks are specific to this challenge rather than generic. First, a thin applicant pool: the RFTP itself notes quantum technologies are not yet widely integrated into biomedical workflows. We mitigate with named-list recruitment and cross-domain matchmaking rather than open calls, using Stage 1 team formation as the early indicator. Second, SME recruitment and retention across a five-year period of performance in a small expert field. We mitigate with an SME bench deeper than the seats we need, staggered terms, and conflict screening refreshed each stage. Third, the RFTP's own internal schedule inconsistency between the May 2030 availability date and the 12/31/2031 completion date. We resolve it conservatively — evaluation and closeout carry through 12/31/2031 — and will confirm intent with the COR at kickoff.
