Project Timeline: The task order runs from award through December 2031, with buffer at each gate for Government approval.

Setup and Launch (award plus eight weeks): Six weeks to finalize rules with NCATS and NASA BPS, build the website, and assemble the reviewer roster. Stage 1 registration opens within eight weeks of award, submissions close mid-2027, up to 25 winners announced by November 2027.

Stage 2 (through March 2030): Milestone 1 submissions due mid-2028, up to 15 winners announced by November 2028. Milestone 2 submissions close late 2029, with the grand prize winner and up to six runners-up announced by March 2030.

Convening and Closeout (May 2030 to December 2031): The convening follows the final announcements around May 2030; all solutions are available by May 31, 2030 per RFTP 3.1. The twelve-month post-award survey and final report close the task order by December 31, 2031. Fixed lead times protect each public date: rules and rubrics approved before a round opens, reviewer recruitment and conflict screening complete 21 days before close, scoring and reconciliation within 30 days.

Key risks are low Stage 1 participation in a community that does not yet exist, recruiting judges credible in both fields, and drift across five years.
